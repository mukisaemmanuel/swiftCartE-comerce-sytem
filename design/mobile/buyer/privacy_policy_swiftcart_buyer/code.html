2. How We Use Your Data
expand_more

Every record collected is utilized strictly to provide lightning-fast, verified local commerce:

  • check Fulfillment: Forwarding your purchase request to verified merchants for fast item picking and packing.
  • check Boda Rider Dispatch: When an order is handed over, your destination drop-off point and direct phone number are shared solely with the assigned courier to enable instant drop-off coordination and door-to-door arrival alerts.
  • check Resolution & Support: SwiftCart customer care agents inspect transaction logs to quickly resolve returns, failed handoffs, or merchant discrepancies.

3. Payment Information
expand_more
lock Payment Integrity Standard

We do not store your Mobile Money PIN or bank card CVV. All MTN MoMo and Airtel Money payments are processed directly through certified telecom payment gateways.

When initiating payment via MTN MoMo or Airtel Money, an encrypted USSD prompt push is initiated directly to your registered handset. SwiftCart systems receive only authenticated authorization tokens and transaction reference IDs, preventing sensitive PIN disclosure at all stages.

my_location
4. Location & Tracking
expand_more

Precise GPS coordinates are utilized only while an active delivery is in transit to facilitate real-time rider navigation through Kampala's winding sectors and regional road networks.

Once your package is marked as Delivered by the Boda rider and confirmed via your OTP or signature, background location sampling terminates immediately. We do not track your perpetual device movements when the SwiftCart app is idle.

cloud_done
5. Data Retention & Security
expand_more

Buyer records are maintained in compliance with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019) and hosted on enterprise-grade ISO 27001-certified cloud infrastructure.

All data in transit is protected via TLS 1.3 encryption, and sensitive databases employ AES-256 field encryption at rest. Audit logs are routinely inspected to guard against unauthorized access, credential stuffing, and illicit merchant leakage.

expand_more

You hold definitive sovereignty over your personal profile. You may at any point exercise your statutory entitlements:

download
Data Export: Request a complete JSON/CSV dossier of your past order transactions, addresses, and account logs.
