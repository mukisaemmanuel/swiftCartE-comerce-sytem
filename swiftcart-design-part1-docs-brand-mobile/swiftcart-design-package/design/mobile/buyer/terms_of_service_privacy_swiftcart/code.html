Escrow Protection

SwiftCart Escrow Guarantee

account_balance_wallet Funds held safely in central vault until you verify your parcel.

All buyer payments conducted via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money Uganda, or Visa/MasterCard are immediately diverted into the SwiftCart Escrow Holding Vault administered under Bank of Uganda National Payments Systems regulations.

Neither the individual merchant nor the logistics rider possesses access to your transaction value until you physically open the package, confirm authenticity, and furnish the delivery PIN.

4
Seller Standards

Merchant Fulfillment & SLA

timer Strict 24-hour dispatch rule & counterfeit eradication program.

Registered Ugandan vendors agree to pack and label orders within twenty-four (24) hours of order placement. Orders failing rider handover within 36 hours automatically default to voluntary order cancellation with immediate full buyer refund.

Counterfeit or imitation goods trigger immediate vendor suspension, permanent account forfeiture, and legal reporting to the Uganda National Bureau of Standards (UNBS).

5
Privacy Shield

Data & Phone Number Masking

phone_in_talk Proxy VoIP calls shield your real telephone number from dispatch couriers.

In full conformity with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019), delivery drivers and merchant handlers never view your private telephone digits.

Calls placed between riders and shoppers are routed via encrypted virtual telecom relays. Once an order finishes delivery, communications channels permanently self-terminate.
