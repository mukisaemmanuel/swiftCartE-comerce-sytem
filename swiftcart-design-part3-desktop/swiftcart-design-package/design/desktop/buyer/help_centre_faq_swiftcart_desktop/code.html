Payments & MTN / Airtel MoMo Rails

Telecom network spikes can occasionally delay the push notification on MTN or Airtel SIM cards. Follow these steps:

MTN MoMo Manual Approval

Dial *165# > Select 'My Account' > 'Pending Approvals' > Enter your MoMo PIN to complete payment.

Airtel Money Manual Push

Dial *185# > Select 'Pending Transactions' > Authorize SwiftCart Escrow reference.

Zero merchant checkout fees. SwiftCart absorbs carrier API gateway charges on all MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money deposits. The price shown on the checkout screen in UGX is the absolute total deducted from your mobile wallet.

This occurs when carrier webhooks experience timeout delays. Our background reconciliation daemon matches telecom reference IDs every 90 seconds.

If your status does not update within 5 minutes, enter your MoMo Transaction ID (e.g. 12849204812) into our automated reconciliation tool in the 'Orders' tab, or ping our WhatsApp support line for instant manual verification.

SwiftCart Escrow Guarantee

Your money never goes directly to an individual vendor's personal account. Payments sit safely in a regulated Trust Escrow Account supervised under the Bank of Uganda National Payments Systems regulatory sandbox.

The vendor cannot withdraw, transfer, or claim these shillings until the buyer hands over the authenticated PIN code upon physical delivery.

Even after providing your PIN code for electronic goods, power tools, or household machinery, merchant payout balance undergoes a 48-hour hold buffer. If the product develops defects or displays hidden non-conformities, clicking "Raise Escrow Dispute" freezes merchant liquidation instantly pending inspection.

Disputes are reviewed by our Nakasero Dispute Review Committee. The committee checks pre-dispatch warehouse footage, serial numbers registered in the system, and buyer unpack photos. 94% of validated non-genuine or damaged claims are decided in favor of the consumer within 24 hours.

Becoming a Verified Merchant

To preserve buyer trust across Uganda, merchants selling on SwiftCart must provide:

  • Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB) Certificate of Incorporation or Business Name Registration.
  • Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) Tax Identification Number (TIN).
  • Valid National ID (NIRA NIN) of the principal proprietor.
  • Physical shop address verification (e.g. Kikuubo, Pioneer Mall, Garden City, or Luwum Street stall contract).

SwiftCart operates on a clear, tiered commission matrix ranging strictly between 8% and 12% on completed orders. There are no monthly listing subscriptions, no catalog fees, and zero hidden platform upkeep charges.

Payouts process automatically on a Daily 5:00 PM EAT cycle directly into your merchant MTN MoMo Pay merchant code, Airtel Pay merchant wallet, or registered commercial bank account (Stanbic, Centenary, Absa) with zero manual withdrawal delays.
