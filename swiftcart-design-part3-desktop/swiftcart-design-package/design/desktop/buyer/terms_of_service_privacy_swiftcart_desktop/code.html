Buyer Escrow Protection Rules

Statutory Custody

SwiftCart operates an enforceable third-party telecom escrow system designed to eliminate digital commerce fraud across Kampala, Wakiso, and national delivery corridors.

lock

Escrow Quarantine Protocols:

Return & Dispute Arbitration Policy

Uganda ETA 2011

Under the Uganda Electronic Transactions Act 2011 (Part IV: Consumer Protection), you are entitled to fair commercial transparency, accurate representation, and structured recourse mechanisms.

timer

48-Hour Post-Delivery Inspection Window

Even after physical OTP entry, buyers possess a statutory 48-hour warranty against latent technical defects, counterfeit parts, or goods deviating fundamentally from listing specifications. Initiating a claim locks merchant funds from wallet payout pending swift dispute triage.

Arbitration Triage Steps:

  1. Dispute Ticket Logged: Buyer uploads high-definition photographic or video proof via SwiftCart portal within 48 hours of dispatch receipt.
  2. Neutral Technical Assessment: SwiftCart inspection hubs in Nakasero review claims alongside the vendor's pre-dispatch manifest within 24 working hours.
  3. Direct Binding Adjudication: If merchant fault is upheld, a full refund plus SafeBoda reverse transit credit is returned automatically to the buyer's MoMo account.
