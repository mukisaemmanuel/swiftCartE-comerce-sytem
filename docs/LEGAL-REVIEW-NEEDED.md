# Legal Review Required Before Launch

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Pre-Launch Mandate:** All platform legal documents, customer terms, merchant agreements, and privacy policies in this repository are drafts created for user interface presentation. A qualified Ugandan commercial lawyer admitted to the Uganda Bar must review and formally endorse these documents prior to commercial operation in Uganda.

---

## 1. Regulatory Status & Licensing Truthfulness
- **Marketplace Model:** SwiftCart Uganda is an online marketplace connecting buyers with third-party sellers. SwiftCart is **not** a bank, credit institution, microfinance deposit-taking institution, or licensed payment system provider.
- **Payment Processing:** All digital payments (MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Visa, Mastercard) are processed through **Pesapal**, a licensed Payment System Provider. SwiftCart does not hold customer funds in statutory escrow or banking custody.
- **No False Claims:** The platform UI and legal texts must never claim licenses, regulatory sandboxes, or supervisory oversight from the Bank of Uganda, statutory NPS Act certifications, or institutional custodian partnerships that do not exist.

---

## 2. Key Areas for Ugandan Legal Counsel Review

### A. Payment Hold & Disbursement Flow
- **Mechanism:** Buyers pay via Pesapal at checkout. Funds are held pending order fulfillment. When the buyer inspects the package and provides their 6-digit delivery code to the courier, delivery is confirmed. After a specified return window (48 hours), funds are disbursed to the seller's account less platform commission.
- **Counsel Review Focus:**
  - Enforceability of the 6-digit delivery confirmation code as evidence of receipt under the Electronic Transactions Act 2011 and Electronic Signatures Act 2011.
  - Clarity of contractual relationship between Buyer, Seller, SwiftCart (Marketplace Platform), and Pesapal (Payment Gateway).
  - Conditions under which payment holds may be extended during active disputes.

### B. Buyer Protection & Dispute Adjudication
- **Dispute Policy:** Buyers may lodge a claim within 48 hours for defective, wrong, or counterfeit items. SwiftCart customer support mediates between buyer and merchant.
- **Counsel Review Focus:**
  - Compliance with the Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act 2017.
  - Neutral arbitration vs. platform mediation limits.
  - Clear allocation of return courier costs for defective goods vs. change-of-mind cancellations.

### C. Courier & Handover Rules
- **SafeBoda & Courier Integration:** Independent dispatch riders deliver parcels across Greater Kampala.
- **Counsel Review Focus:**
  - Threshold of risk transfer: Risk passes when buyer inspects parcel and reveals the 6-digit code.
  - Prohibition against couriers demanding codes over telephone or WhatsApp prior to physical doorstep delivery.
  - Platform liability limits regarding courier delays, loss, or transit damage.

### D. Merchant Obligations & Prohibited Goods
- **Seller Policies:** All sellers must register with verifiable business identification (URSB registration, National ID, physical address).
- **Prohibited Goods:**
  - Counterfeit, cloned, or replica electronics.
  - Unregistered or prescription pharmaceuticals without NDA permits.
  - Firearms, weapons, and hazardous chemical materials.
  - Expired foods, illicit substances, and banned wildlife products.
- **Counsel Review Focus:**
  - Indemnification clauses protecting SwiftCart from merchant intellectual property infringements and defective products.
  - Procedures for account suspension, blacklist, and reporting unlawful merchandise to relevant Ugandan authorities.

### E. Data Privacy & Consumer Protection
- **Data Collection:** Phone numbers, full names, delivery coordinates/addresses, order history, and device telemetry.
- **Data Sharing:** Restricted strictly to payment gateway (Pesapal), SMS delivery aggregators, and assigned delivery couriers (masked where feasible).
- **Counsel Review Focus:**
  - Alignment with the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 and Data Protection and Privacy Regulations 2021.
  - Formal registration with the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) as a data collector/processor prior to launch.
  - User rights to access, rectify, and delete personal data.

### F. Limitation of Liability
- **Marketplace Disclaimer:** SwiftCart disclaims warranties regarding merchant listings, product merchantability, and third-party telecom network outages, subject to mandatory statutory consumer rights under Ugandan law.
- **Counsel Review Focus:**
  - Enforceability of financial liability caps (limited to the value of the disputed order).
  - Severability and governing law (Laws of the Republic of Uganda; jurisdiction in the High Court of Uganda at Kampala).
