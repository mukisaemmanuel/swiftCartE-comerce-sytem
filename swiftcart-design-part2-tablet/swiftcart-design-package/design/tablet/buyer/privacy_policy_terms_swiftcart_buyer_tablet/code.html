1. SwiftCart Data Pledge & Escrow Overview

SwiftCart operates a high-throughput multi-vendor digital commerce marketplace serving buyers across Uganda. To protect all marketplace participants, our data protocols are anchored in real-time escrow safety. When you authorize an order, your capital and personal contact coordinates are ring-fenced inside our secured operational vault until verified delivery handover by your assigned courier.

account_balance_wallet Payment Escrow Protection

Payment deductions via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, or Card remain in an independent trustee escrow until goods pass physical buyer inspection at the door.

visibility_off Anonymized Merchant Handoff

Independent retail merchants never receive your direct mobile number or payment identifiers—only warehouse SKU manifests and delivery tracking codes.

4. Payment Security & Zero PIN Storage

SwiftCart integrates directly with Bank of Uganda licensed National Payment System (NPS) operators, including MTN Mobile Money Uganda and Airtel Money. We strictly maintain a Zero Mobile Money PIN Storage Architecture.

warning
Critical Consumer Payment Warning

SwiftCart staff, agents, and Boda couriers will NEVER prompt, request, or input your 4-digit or 5-digit Mobile Money PIN. PIN entry occurs exclusively via official telecom USSD prompts (*165# or *185#) or certified financial SDK webhooks on your personal device.

PCI-DSS Tokenized Card digits are never kept on local disk. Encrypted tokens only.
BOU NPS Authorized Settlement partners operate with full central bank licensure.
Instant Chargebacks Automated dispute mediation within 24 hours for nondelivery.

In compliance with Part IV of Uganda's Data Protection Act, every buyer possesses full statutory rights over their personal telemetry:

download Right of Portability
