Payments & Mobile Money Escrow

Bank of Uganda regulatory framework and MoMo prompts

2 Articles

Every UGX sent through MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money is held in a ring-fenced custodial escrow trust regulated under the National Payment Systems Act 2020.

Funds are never transferred to the independent merchant until you confirm inspection of goods via your 6-digit delivery PIN or after a 48-hour inspection period passes without an active dispute.

If the automated push prompt times out on your handset:

  1. Dial *165# on your MTN line immediately.
  2. Select Option 4 (Payments) → Option 6 (Pending Approvals).
  3. Authorize the transaction labeled SwiftCart Uganda Escrow.
  4. If it fails, tap "Retry Payment" on checkout within 15 minutes.
